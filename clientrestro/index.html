About Us

At Spice & Soul, we bring you the rich, diverse culinary heritage of India in a modern, minimalistic setting. Our chefs use the finest ingredients and traditional spices to craft dishes that nourish both the body and the soul. Experience dining stripped of distractions, focusing purely on taste, aroma, and connection.

Our Signature Dishes

Butter Chicken

Tender chicken simmered in a velvety tomato and butter gravy, infused with aromatic spices.

Paneer Tikka Masala
